# CR1515
CR1515 — Offline Peer-to-Peer Communication for Crisis Zones
Problems CR1515 Tackles:

Internet shutdowns/blackouts cut off communication entirely - no way to reach people during civil unrest or state-imposed blocks
Cell towers overloaded, damaged, or deliberately shut down during disasters/protests - SMS and calls fail
Existing chat apps (WhatsApp, Messenger, etc.) require internet - useless once data blocked
No backup communication channel for ordinary people without dedicated radio/satellite hardware
Emergency info (shelter locations, danger zones, safety alerts) can't spread once networks go down
People in same physical area (protest, disaster site, shelter) have no way to coordinate locally without infra
Centralized servers = single point of failure and censorship - authorities can block/monitor them
Verified information can't be distinguished from rumor once official channels are down
